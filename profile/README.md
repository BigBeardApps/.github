One-person studio run by [Raul C. Peña](https://bigbeardapps.com/about.html) out of Georgetown, Texas. Air Force veteran, 20+ years in Texas real estate, self-taught coder. Every app is built on one idea: **your data belongs on your device.** No ads, no tracking, no accounts, nothing sold to anyone.

## The apps

| App | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="icons/feastmark.png" width="28" height="28" alt=""> **[Feastmark](https://feastmark.app/)** | Turns recipe videos from Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube into your personal cookbook — even when the recipe is only spoken aloud. | **[Live](https://apps.apple.com/app/feastmark-save-any-recipe/id6762322193)** |
| <img src="icons/payoffpilot.png" width="28" height="28" alt=""> **[PayoffPilot](https://getpayoffpilot.app/)** | A private debt-payoff planner. Compare Snowball vs. Avalanche with your real numbers — pay once, never subscribe. | **[Live](https://apps.apple.com/app/payoffpilot/id6753783449)** |
| <img src="icons/reeltalk.png" width="28" height="28" alt=""> **[ReelTalk](https://bigbeardapps.com/reeltalk/)** | 30 original fishing-pun stickers for iMessage. Big bass energy for your group chats. | **[Live](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/reel-talk-fishing-stickers/id6794788951)** |
| <img src="icons/gunmark.png" width="28" height="28" alt=""> **[Gunmark](https://gunmark.app/)** | A private firearm collection catalog — guided photos, build history, receipts and tax stamps on your device. | **[Live](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gunmark/id6795672837)** |
| <img src="icons/huntmark.png" width="28" height="28" alt=""> **[HuntMark](https://huntmark.app/)** | Public land hunting, by species — WMAs, State Parks, National Forests, Army Corps and more. Spots stay on the device. | **[Join the beta](https://huntmark.app/beta)** |
